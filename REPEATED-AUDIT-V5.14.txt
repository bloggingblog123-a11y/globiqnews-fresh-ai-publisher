LIMITATIONS THAT CANNOT BE HONESTLY GUARANTEED BY STATIC CODE
- A publisher may change its HTML/RSS or block server-side access at any time.
- Rank Math's power-word list is internal and regularly updated, so a server-side clone cannot guarantee that every future Rank Math version recognizes the same word.
- Rank Math Content AI is separate from normal Rank Math SEO analysis.
- Live hosting/PHP memory/API quota behavior requires one real WordPress test after installation.
