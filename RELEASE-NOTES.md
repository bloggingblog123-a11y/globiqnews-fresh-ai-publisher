# GlobiqNews Fresh AI Publisher 6.1.2

Responds to the remaining short-body, title-sentiment/power-word and internal-link warnings in 6.1.1. Title generation and repair now try accurate, evidence-supported descriptors using vocabulary examples from the installed Rank Math power-word list. Neutral titles remain valid when sentiment cannot be justified. A title improvement cannot conceal a substantial reduction of an already-short body.

Internal linking now recognizes a specific subject whose keyword terms occur together in different wording in an existing published article. It still excludes private, password-protected, unpublished, self and unrelated targets, respects OFF and inserts at most three links. No arbitrary link is added just for a score.

Improve SEO & Links reports the saved body length, title checks and internal-link result, plus evidence or configuration blockers. Short-output reasons are retained even when a proposed rewrite is unchanged. A one-time new repair budget for unchanged generated Drafts is limited to three attempts.

Update in WordPress without deleting the plugin. For an existing generated Draft, open GlobiqNews Original Content Report, click Improve SEO & Links, then reopen the editor. Installation alone does not rewrite old articles. Manual edits remain protected. All articles remain Draft; images and external-link controls are unchanged.

The controlled 385-word fixture expanded to 650 words and passed actual Rank Math Free 1.0.278 sentiment, power-word and internal-link checks; its under-600 warning cleared. A negative fixture rejected an unsupported positive title. These are local tests with controlled AI/research responses, not verification of the user's production draft. Limited evidence, failed quality review, unavailable AI or no relevant published posts can still leave warnings. See TEST-REPORT-6.1.2.md.
