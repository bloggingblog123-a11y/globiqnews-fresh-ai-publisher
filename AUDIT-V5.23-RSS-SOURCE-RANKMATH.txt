RSS / ATOM

[PASS] Existing WordPress SimplePie parser, raw fallback, recursion guard and blocked-source rules remain.

SOURCE / CATEGORY URL
[PASS] A child section URL cannot become an article candidate only because it lives under the category path.
[PASS] Normal candidates need an actual article URL signal, or a clear editorial-card/headline context for flat publisher permalinks.

[PASS] Short listing URLs such as /technology.html are no longer treated as direct articles merely because they end in .html and have an H1.
[PASS] Direct .html/.cms articles can still pass when article-specific evidence exists.
[PASS] V5.22 category-feed title scoping and longest genuine JSON-LD articleBody extraction remain.

RANK MATH SEO
